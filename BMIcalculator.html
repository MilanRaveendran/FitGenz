What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a numerical value calculated using a person's weight and height.

BMI Categories

  • Below 18.5 → Underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9 → Normal
  • 25 – 29.9 → Overweight
  • 30+ → Obese

BMI is a general indicator and not a medical diagnosis.
