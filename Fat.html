What is Body Fat %?

Body fat percentage is the amount of fat compared to total body weight.

Formula Used

Men:
495 / (1.0324 − 0.19077 log10(waist − neck) + 0.15456 log10(height)) − 450

Women:
495 / (1.29579 − 0.35004 log10(waist + hip − neck) + 0.22100 log10(height)) − 450

How It Works

This formula uses body measurements to estimate fat distribution.

Healthy Range

  • Men: 10% – 20%
  • Women: 18% – 28%
