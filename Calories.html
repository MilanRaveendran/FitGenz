What are Calories?

Calories are units of energy that your body uses to perform all daily activities such as breathing, walking, and exercising.

How Calories are Calculated

Step 1: BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)

Men: BMR = 10×weight + 6.25×height − 5×age + 5
Women: BMR = 10×weight + 6.25×height − 5×age − 161

Step 2: Total Daily Energy (TDEE)

TDEE = BMR × Activity Level

Activity Levels

  • Sedentary → Little or no exercise
  • Light → 1–3 days/week
  • Moderate → 3–5 days/week
  • Very Active → 6–7 days/week
  • Extra Active → Hard training/physical job

Goal-Based Calories

  • Maintain Weight → TDEE
  • Weight Loss → TDEE − 500 kcal
  • Weight Gain → TDEE + 500 kcal

Important Notes

  • Calories are an estimate, not exact
  • Metabolism differs per person
  • Use this as a guideline, not medical advice
